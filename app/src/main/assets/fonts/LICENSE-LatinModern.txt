Latin Modern fonts (TrueType build)

The bundled LatinModernRoman, LatinModernSans and LatinModernMono TrueType
files in this directory are converted from the Latin Modern OpenType fonts
produced by the GUST e-foundry (Boguslaw Jackowski and Janusz M. Nowacki),
themselves based on Donald E. Knuth's Computer Modern. The outlines were
converted from cubic (CFF) to quadratic (glyf) curves for embedding; no
glyphs were redrawn.
